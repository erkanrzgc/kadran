# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about code, not about people. Assume the other person is acting
in good faith until they clearly aren't.

## What's expected

- Give and take technical criticism directly. A blunt "this is wrong because X"
  is welcome; "you are careless" is not.
- Back claims with evidence. In this project especially, "I measured it and here
  is the output" ends arguments that opinions cannot.
- Accept that maintainer time is finite. Unanswered issues are a backlog, not a
  snub.

## What's not

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments — including about someone's
  experience level, language, nationality, gender, or background.
- Sexualized language or imagery, in any context.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or repeatedly reopening settled decisions
  without new evidence.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any space
where you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **benerkanrzgc@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately and
the reporter's identity is not disclosed.

Responses escalate as warranted: a private correction, a public one, a temporary
block, or a permanent ban. The maintainer makes this call and will explain the
reasoning to the person affected.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
